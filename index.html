THE BRAND

Meet BioBrew.

BioBrew is a student-created concept that connects traditional fermented beverages with fundamental biotechnology. Our focus is the fermentation of a rice-based substrate using yeast.

The project demonstrates the roles of starch conversion, microbial metabolism, hygiene and fermentation monitoring in product development.
