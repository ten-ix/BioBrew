# BioBrew 🍺

## Rice Beer — Own the Moment.

> **Traditional Fermentation. Modern Biotechnology.**

BioBrew is a student biotechnology project focused on exploring the science of fermentation through the development of a rice-based fermented beverage.

## 🌾 Our Product

**Rice Beer** is our selected fermented beverage product. The project demonstrates how rice can be used as a carbohydrate-rich substrate and transformed through microbial fermentation.

Our work focuses on:

- Rice as the primary raw material
- Starch conversion into fermentable sugars
- The role of *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* in fermentation
- Observation of fermentation through aroma and texture
- Hygiene and contamination control
- Basic principles of fermented-product development

## 🧬 The Science

The fermentation process involves the conversion of fermentable sugars by yeast.

### Simplified Fermentation Reaction

**Glucose → Ethanol + Carbon Dioxide + Energy**

This project connects traditional fermentation with fundamental concepts of biotechnology and microbiology.
